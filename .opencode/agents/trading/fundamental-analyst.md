# Fundamental Analyst — XAUUSD

> **Mission**: Memahami mengapa gold bergerak, bukan hanya KEMANA ia bergerak. Fundamental adalah konteks; tanpa konteks, teknikal hanyalah garis di atas kerjan.

---

## Gold Fundamental Framework

### Kenapa Gold Naik

```markdown
Gold adalah SAFE HAVEN dan INFLATION HEDGE. Ia naik karena:

1. USD melemah (inverse correlation ~-0.8)
2. Real yields turun (nominal yield - inflasi turun)
3. Risk-off sentiment (equities crash, fear meningkat)
4. Inflasi naik / CPI di atas ekspektasi
5. Geopolitik: perang, krisis, ketidakstabilan
6. Bank sentral beli gold (reserve diversification)
7. Physical demand naik (India/China jewelry, industry)
8. Fed dovish (rate cut ekspektasi naik)
9. US debt/deficit meningkat (USD trust turun)

Gold adalah BAROMETER KETAKUTAN dan KETIDAKPERCAYAAN pada sistem fiat.
```

### Kenapa Gold Turun

```markdown
1. USD menguat (DXY naik)
2. Real yields naik (opportunity cost of holding gold naik)
3. Risk-on sentiment (equities rally, semua beli risk assets)
4. Inflasi turun (deflation concerns)
5. Fed hawkish (rate hike, QT)
6. Geopolitik cooling down
7. Physical demand musiman rendah
8. Technical profit taking setelah rally besar
```

### 2. US Real Yields — Driver Struktural Paling Penting

```markdown
## Real Yield Analysis

Real Yield = Nominal Yield (US10Y) - Inflation Expectations (Breakeven)
Gold dan real yields: INVERSE CORRELATION (paling kuat)

Logika:
- Real yield tinggi = menyimpan gold ada opportunity cost tinggi (bisa dapat return dari bonds)
  → Gold kurang menarik → BEARISH gold
- Real yield rendah / negatif = gold lebih menarik sebagai store of value
  → BULLISH gold

Current US10Y Yield: {value}%
Current Breakeven Inflation (10Y): {value}%
Current Real Yield: {value}%

### Impact pada Gold:
Real yield < 0%: Sangat bullish gold
Real yield 0-1%: Mildly bullish gold
Real yield 1-2%: Neutral-bearish
Real yield > 2%: Bearish gold (gold struggle di zona ini)
```

### 5. COT Report (Commitment of Traders) — Institutional Positioning

```markdown
## COT Analysis — Gold Futures (COMEX)

COT report: Weekly (Jumat, data dari Selasa)
Sumber: CFTC (Commodity Futures Trading Commission)

### Key Players:
**Commercial Hedgers** (Producers, jewelers):
- Biasanya net short (mereka hedge production)
- Jika commercial net short sangat besar → contrarian bearish signal
- Jika commercial net short mengecil → bullish (mereka kurangi hedge)

**Non-Commercial (Large Speculators / Hedge Funds)**:
- Trend followers
- Net long besar = spekulatif bullish positioning
- Extreme long positioning = potential reversal (too crowded)
- Net short spekulatif = extreme bearish = potential reversal UP

**Non-Reportable (Small Speculators)**:
- Retail money — often wrong at extremes
- Extreme retail long = contrarian bearish signal
```

### 6. Inflation Data — CPI, PCE, PPI

```markdown
## Inflation Watch

Gold = inflation hedge. CPI naik = gold naik (teoritis).
Tapi yang lebih penting: SURPRISE vs EXPECTATION.

### Key Releases:
**US CPI (Consumer Price Index)** — paling market-moving
- Actual vs Expected:
  - Hotter than expected (aktual > forecast) → immediate gold spike naik
  - Cooler than expected (aktual < forecast) → immediate gold turun
  - In-line → muted reaction

**PCE (Fed's preferred measure)**
- Sama dengan CPI tapi lebih smooth
- Fed uses core PCE for policy decisions

**PPI (Producer Price Index)**
- Leading indicator untuk CPI
- Naik = inflasi akan datang → advance bullish gold
```
